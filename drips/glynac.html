Who provides and monitors my infusion?

Dr. Violeta Aronov personally administers and monitors every infusion. Before it begins, she reviews your medical information, confirms the appropriate formulation and dose, prepares the medications, and sets the infusion rate.

From the initial medical review through the completion of your infusion, every stage remains under her direct care.

What happens before I can book?

Every infusion begins with a physician eligibility review to make sure it is appropriate for your medical history, medications, current health and goals. To begin the process, a $100 physician review deposit is collected. You will then complete the safety screening and medical intake forms for Dr. Aronov to review, along with any relevant laboratory results or medical records.

Once the review is complete, you will receive an email explaining your eligibility and next steps. If approved, the full $100 deposit will be applied toward your infusion. If you are not approved, the deposit covers the physician’s completed review and is not refundable. Completing the review process does not guarantee approval or reserve an appointment.

I am already under specialist care. Does that rule me out?

No. Being under specialist care does not automatically rule you out. Eligibility depends on whether glycine, N-acetylcysteine and IV hydration are appropriate for your current health.

Absolute contraindications Specific to N-acetylcysteine:
  • A previous hypersensitivity reaction to N-acetylcysteine or another ingredient in the formulation
Specific to glycine:
  • A previous hypersensitivity reaction to glycine
  • Nonketotic hyperglycinemia — a rare disorder in which glycine accumulates in the body
  • Another diagnosed disorder affecting glycine, amino-acid or nitrogen metabolism
Related to the combined infusion and IV fluid:
  • Anuria — an inability to produce urine — or severe kidney failure
  • Advanced liver failure with hepatic encephalopathy or elevated ammonia
  • Active pulmonary edema, decompensated heart failure, or another condition that makes IV hydration unsafe
Relative contraindications and cautions

These conditions do not automatically rule you out, but they require additional medical review:

  • Asthma or a history of bronchospasm, particularly if poorly controlled
  • A history of severe allergies or a previous infusion reaction
  • Stable kidney, liver, heart or lung disease
  • A condition requiring fluid restriction
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Active chemotherapy, radiation, or an upcoming major procedure
  • Medications that may interact with the ingredients or affect blood pressure, clotting or amino-acid metabolism
Why only three components?

Because GlyNAC is a focused precursor formula. Glutathione is made from three amino acids:

glutamate + cysteine + glycine → glutathione

NAC is converted into cysteine. Cysteine is commonly the rate-limiting building block, and glycine may also become insufficient in older adults.

GlyNAC supplies glycine and cysteine through NAC — the two building blocks that research suggests may become limited with age. Glutamate is generally available through food and normal metabolism, and the body can also produce it from glutamine. The hydration fluid serves as the IV carrier.

Other nutrients may support related antioxidant pathways, but they are not additional glutathione building blocks and are not part of the specific GlyNAC combination studied in the research.

Will this slow down aging?

Current research has not established that GlyNAC slows aging or extends lifespan in people. Human studies have reported improvements in several measures associated with healthy aging, including glutathione status, mitochondrial function, oxidative stress, inflammation and physical performance.

Animal studies have also shown improvements in aging-related pathways, with one mouse study reporting longer lifespan. These findings are promising, but they do not prove a human longevity effect, and the published studies did not evaluate this IV formulation.

GlyNAC is therefore offered to support biological pathways associated with healthy aging — not as an age-reversal or lifespan-extension therapy.

What does the $100 physician consultation include?

The $100 physician consultation includes reviewing your medical history, medications and relevant laboratory results. With your permission, Dr. Aronov will also contact your primary care physician — and your specialist when appropriate — to confirm whether GlyNAC is medically appropriate for you.

Scheduling occurs only after the safety review is complete. If approved, the $100 consultation fee is applied to your first infusion.

What if I’m not eligible?

Not every patient can safely receive every infusion. If Dr. Aronov determines that GlyNAC Healthy Aging IV is not appropriate for you — or cannot be provided safely within the capabilities of this outpatient practice — she will explain the reason clearly.

This does not mean that your concerns are being dismissed. Depending on the reason, the safest next step may be additional testing, evaluation by your primary-care physician or specialist, a different supportive option, or no infusion at all. The purpose of the review is to make the safest medical decision for you.
