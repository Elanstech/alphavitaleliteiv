Why each component is included

What’s included — and the role of each component.

ImmunoBoost is administered in multiple stages and includes vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and antioxidants. These ingredients support different aspects of normal immune function, cellular metabolism, tissue maintenance, and antioxidant defenses. The sections below identify the individual ingredients in each category and explain their biological roles.

  1. 01

    Essential trace minerals

    Zinc, copper, manganese and selenium

    These minerals are needed in small amounts, but each supports a different part of normal immune and antioxidant function:

    • Zinc supports immune-cell development, communication and normal responses. It is also required for protein and DNA synthesis, cell division and wound healing.
    • Copper contributes to normal immune function, iron metabolism and connective-tissue formation. It is also part of copper–zinc superoxide dismutase, an enzyme that helps protect cells from oxidative damage.
    • Manganese is part of mitochondrial superoxide dismutase, an antioxidant enzyme that helps protect the mitochondria where cells produce energy.
    • Selenium is incorporated into selenoproteins, including glutathione peroxidases and thioredoxin reductases, which help regulate oxidative stress and support normal immune function.

    These minerals are included in carefully selected amounts because trace-mineral balance matters, and more is not necessarily better.

  2. 02

    Cellular energy and immune-cell renewal

    B-complex vitamins

    This B-complex blend includes B1, B2, B3, B5, B6 and B12. These vitamins perform different but connected functions:

    • B1, B2, B3 and B5 help cells convert nutrients into usable energy.
    • B2 and B3 also participate in cellular redox and antioxidant pathways.
    • B6 supports amino-acid metabolism and normal immune-cell function.
    • B12 is required for DNA synthesis and normal blood-cell formation.

    Together, these vitamins support the energy production and cell renewal required for a normal immune response.

  3. 03

    Metabolic and immune-cell cofactor

    Magnesium

    Magnesium is required by hundreds of enzymes involved in cellular energy production, protein and DNA synthesis and electrolyte regulation. Magnesium signaling also participates in normal immune-cell activity, including T-cell activation and antibody-related pathways. It is included as a metabolic and cellular-signaling cofactor.

  4. 04

    Cellular signaling

    Calcium gluconate

    Calcium is more than a bone mineral. Inside immune cells, it acts as a signaling messenger. Carefully regulated calcium signals help lymphocytes activate, multiply and communicate during a normal immune response. Calcium gluconate provides calcium in a form suitable for intravenous administration.

  5. 05

    Protein and tissue building blocks

    AminoMultiPlex amino-acid blend

    This blend contains four amino acids with different biological roles:

    • L-arginine supports protein production and is a precursor to nitric oxide, a signaling molecule involved in circulation, immune-cell activity and tissue repair.
    • L-glutamine provides fuel and nitrogen for rapidly dividing cells, including immune cells and the cells lining the intestinal tract.
    • L-lysine is an essential amino acid required for protein production, collagen formation and tissue maintenance.
    • L-proline is a major structural amino acid in collagen and connective tissue.

    Together, these amino acids provide building materials used for normal immune-cell turnover, protein synthesis and tissue maintenance.

  6. 06

    Mitochondrial and antioxidant support

    Alpha-lipoic acid

    Alpha-lipoic acid works as a cofactor for mitochondrial enzymes that help cells process fuel. Its reduced form interacts with vitamin C and glutathione within the body’s antioxidant network. It is included to support cellular redox balance, including within metabolically active immune cells.

  7. 07

    Immune, antioxidant and collagen support

    Vitamin C

    Humans cannot produce vitamin C and must obtain it from outside sources. Vitamin C supports the body’s protective tissue barriers, normal white-blood-cell function and cellular antioxidant defenses. It is also required for collagen production and normal wound healing. Adequate vitamin C is necessary for normal innate and adaptive immune function.

  8. 08

    Intracellular antioxidant defense

    Glutathione

    Glutathione is produced naturally inside the body’s cells and is one of the body’s principal intracellular antioxidants. It helps maintain the redox balance immune cells need for normal signaling, DNA synthesis and lymphocyte function. It also supports enzyme systems that neutralize peroxides and other reactive compounds. It is included for cellular antioxidant support.

They do not establish that the combined ImmunoBoost IV protocol improves immune function, prevents or treats infection, or produces a particular clinical outcome. Adequate nutrient status is important for normal immunity, but providing additional nutrients does not necessarily enhance immunity in someone who is not deficient. Before you get in touch

Questions about
this infusion.

Who provides and monitors my infusion?

Dr. Violeta Aronov personally administers and monitors every infusion. Before it begins, she reviews your medical information, confirms the appropriate formulation and dose, prepares the medications, and sets the infusion rate.

From the initial medical review through the completion of your infusion, every stage remains under her direct care.

What happens before I can book?

Every infusion begins with a physician eligibility review to make sure it is appropriate for your medical history, medications, current health and goals. To begin the process, a $100 physician review deposit is collected. You will then complete the safety screening and medical intake forms for Dr. Aronov to review, along with any relevant laboratory results or medical records.

Once the review is complete, you will receive an email explaining your eligibility and next steps. If approved, the full $100 deposit will be applied toward your infusion. If you are not approved, the deposit covers the physician’s completed review and is not refundable. Completing the review process does not guarantee approval or reserve an appointment.

Can I be considered for ImmunoBoost while under a specialist’s care?

Yes. Being under a specialist’s care does not automatically rule you out. Dr. Aronov will review your diagnosis, medications, current treatment and overall health to determine whether the infusion is medically appropriate.

If additional information is needed, she may request relevant records or coordinate with your specialist. ImmunoBoost is supportive care and does not replace or change the treatment prescribed by your specialist.

Will this prevent me from getting sick?

No. ImmunoBoost IV Support is designed to support normal immune function when you are well. It cannot prevent infection or guarantee that you will not become sick.

This infusion is offered solely as adjunctive wellness support. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any disease or medical condition, and has not been evaluated by the FDA for those purposes. It does not replace vaccination, antivirals, antibiotics or care from your physician.

If you are acutely unwell, contact your physician. ImmunoBoost is intended for proactive support — not for treating an active illness.

Why every two to four weeks rather than weekly?

When medically appropriate, ImmunoBoost may be considered approximately every two to four weeks, with timing sometimes closer to twice monthly during flu season. It is intended as proactive wellness support when you are well — not as treatment during an acute illness.

The formulation contains nutrients involved in normal immune-cell development, maturation, function and antioxidant defense. More frequent infusions are not automatically more beneficial. The appropriate interval may change with your health, medications, the season and your response to a previous infusion.

For that reason, Dr. Aronov reviews a new screening before every visit rather than placing patients on an automatic schedule.

What if I’m not eligible?

Not every patient can safely receive every infusion. If Dr. Aronov determines that Immun-O-Boost IV Support is not appropriate for you — or cannot be provided safely within the capabilities of this outpatient practice — she will explain the reason clearly.

This does not mean that your concerns are being dismissed. Depending on the reason, the safest next step may be additional testing, evaluation by your primary-care physician or specialist, a different supportive option, or no infusion at all. The purpose of the review is to make the safest medical decision for you.

ImmunoBoost IV Support is offered as adjunctive wellness support for medically appropriate patients who are well at the time of infusion. It is designed to support hydration and provide nutrients involved in normal immune-cell function and antioxidant defenses.

This infusion is not intended for use during an acute illness and does not replace vaccination, primary or specialist care, prescription medications, antibiotics, antivirals, emergency care or other medically necessary treatment.
