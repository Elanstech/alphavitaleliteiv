Before you get in touch

Questions about
this infusion.

Who provides and monitors my infusion?

Dr. Violeta Aronov personally administers and monitors every infusion. Before it begins, she reviews your medical information, confirms the appropriate formulation and dose, prepares the medications, and sets the infusion rate.

From the initial medical review through the completion of your infusion, every stage remains under her direct care.

What happens before I can book?

Every infusion begins with a physician eligibility review to make sure it is appropriate for your medical history, medications, current health and goals. To begin the process, a $100 physician review fee is collected. You will then complete the safety screening and medical intake forms for Dr. Aronov to review, along with any relevant laboratory results or medical records.

Once the review is complete, you will receive an email explaining your eligibility and next steps. If approved, the full $100 deposit will be applied toward your infusion. If you are not approved, the deposit covers the physician’s completed review and is not refundable. Completing the review process does not guarantee approval or reserve an appointment.

I’m already receiving care from a specialist. Can I still be considered?

Yes. Being under a specialist’s care does not automatically rule you out. Dr. Aronov will review your diagnosis, medications and current treatment plan to determine whether LIQUIXO Muscle Support is appropriate for you.

When coordination is medically appropriate — and with your permission — she will make a reasonable effort to contact your treating specialist. Written approval is not routinely required. This infusion is supportive and does not replace or alter the care prescribed by your specialist.

Do I still need to train and eat properly?

Yes. Supplying amino acids does not substitute for the training stimulus or the diet that muscle maintenance depends on. This is supportive nutritional care alongside those things, not instead of them.

It is not intended to treat muscle loss, sarcopenia or any musculoskeletal condition.

What if I’m not eligible?

Not every patient can safely receive every infusion. If Dr. Aronov determines that LIQUIXO Muscle Recovery IV is not appropriate for you — or cannot be provided safely within the capabilities of this outpatient practice — she will explain the reason clearly.

This does not mean that your concerns are being dismissed. Depending on the reason, the safest next step may be additional testing, evaluation by your primary-care physician or specialist, a different supportive option, or no infusion at all. The purpose of the review is to make the safest medical decision for you.
