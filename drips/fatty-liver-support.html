Liver health is not a one-day detox.

Your liver works around the clock, and one infusion cannot reverse months or years of metabolic stress. The six-week program is a consistent, physician-supervised period of supportive care — not a quick fix.

Everything absorbed from your gut is routed through the liver before it reaches the rest of you. That is why liver health sits at the centre of how the body handles anything you take in, and why this is the longest infusion on the menu.

The six-week structure also gives Dr. Aronov something a single visit cannot: time to monitor tolerance, review your response, and adjust when medically appropriate.

  • 01Carefully selected hydration
  • 02Magnesium, trace minerals and B-complex — involved in normal cellular energy and enzyme function
  • 03Glycine and taurine — amino acids the liver uses to prepare bile, the digestive fluid that helps your body process fats
  • 04N-acetylcysteine and glycine — building blocks the body uses to make glutathione
  • 05Alpha-lipoic acid, vitamin C and glutathione — supporting the body’s natural antioxidant defenses against oxidative stress

What is in the bag

Ten components,
in one order.

Every component is named, and every line says what that component is involved in — not what the infusion will do for you. Read down and watch the bag fill.

0 per cent composed

  1. 01

    Hydration fluid

    The carrier everything else arrives in, and supportive care in its own right. It is also what makes a three-hour infusion a three-hour infusion — the rate is set slow on purpose.

  2. 02

    Magnesium

    Supports hundreds of enzyme reactions, including cellular energy production, protein synthesis, normal muscle and nerve function, and the body’s own production of glutathione. It provides the metabolic foundation that lets the rest of the bag be processed and used.

  3. 03

    Trace minerals

    Cofactors the rest of the formulation depends on. Zinc and copper are kept in ratio deliberately, since excess zinc interferes with copper absorption.

    • Zinc
    • Copper
    • Manganese
    • Selenium
  4. 04

    Vitamin B complex

    B1 through B12, involved in normal energy metabolism and amino-acid metabolism. They help cells convert nutrients into usable energy, and support healthy blood-cell formation and nervous-system function.

    • B1 Thiamine
    • B2 Riboflavin
    • B3 Niacinamide
    • B5 Dexpanthenol
    • B6 Pyridoxine
    • B12 Cyanocobalamin
  5. 05

    Glycine

    One of the amino acids the liver uses to prepare bile — the digestive fluid that helps your body process fats. It is also the second building component of glutathione, which is why it appears twice in the logic of this bag.

  6. 06

    Taurine

    The other amino acid involved in the liver’s normal bile work. Bile is how fats get handled, which is the part of liver function this formulation is built around.

  7. 07

    N-Acetylcysteine

    Supplies cysteine — one of the three amino acids the body needs to make glutathione, and usually the one in shortest supply. With glycine already in the bag, two of the three building blocks are present together.

  8. 08

    Alpha-lipoic acid

    Participates in mitochondrial energy metabolism and in the interconnected antioxidant network that includes vitamin C and glutathione — the three of them recharge one another rather than working alone.

  9. 09

    Vitamin C

    An antioxidant that is also required for normal collagen production. Inside this bag it is the third member of the antioxidant network, alongside alpha-lipoic acid and glutathione.

  10. 10

    Glutathione

    A major antioxidant naturally produced inside the body’s cells. It helps maintain normal cellular redox balance and works with selenium-dependent enzymes as part of the body’s antioxidant defense system.

Think of this program as providing some of the nutritional tools involved in the liver’s normal work — not as doing that work for it.

Component descriptions state what a compound is involved in, not what an infusion will do for you. Supportive ingredients are selected and finalized by Dr. Aronov during physician screening, based on your individual history and clinical appropriateness.

Before you get in touch

Questions about
this infusion.

Will this treat my fatty liver?

No. This is supportive care, offered solely as adjunctive wellness support. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, prevent or reverse any disease or medical condition, and it has not been evaluated by the FDA for those purposes.

The evidence behind fatty liver disease is metabolic — weight, glycemic control and alcohol. This is not a detox and not a shortcut, and Dr. Aronov will tell you so directly.

Why does it take three hours?

Because the rate is set slow deliberately. This is the longest infusion on the menu and carries the most components; Dr. Aronov sets the flow rate at the start, adjusts it through the session, and stays in the room until it is finished.

Can I book a single session instead of the program?

Yes. A single session is $850. The six-week program exists because liver support is not a one-day event, but nothing obliges you to start with six.

Programs are offered only where Dr. Aronov considers repeat sessions appropriate for you.

What happens before I can book?

You send your medical history, current medications and allergies. Dr. Aronov reads it herself and decides whether IV therapy is appropriate for you and which formulation fits. Nothing is booked and nothing is charged at that stage.

The consultation is $100, is required before scheduling, and is applied toward your infusion if she approves one.

Who actually gives the infusion?

Dr. Aronov does, personally. She reviews your history, calculates the doses, prepares the medications, spikes the bag, sets the flow rate, and stays with you until it is finished. There is no technician and nothing is delegated.

Exactly what is in it?

Ten components, each described in full further up this page:

  • Hydration fluid
  • Magnesium
  • Trace minerals — zinc, copper, manganese, selenium
  • Vitamin B complex
  • Glycine and taurine
  • N-acetylcysteine
  • Alpha-lipoic acid, vitamin C and glutathione

Supportive ingredients are selected and finalized during physician screening, based on your individual history and clinical appropriateness.

What if I am not eligible?

Not everyone is. If IV therapy is not appropriate for you, Dr. Aronov will say so and tell you why.
