The formulation

Everything in it,
and nothing else.

No proprietary blend, no undisclosed filler. Ten components, each one named, each one mixed by the physician who administers it.

  1. 01

    Hydration fluid

    The carrier, and the reason this one runs three hours.

  2. 02

    Magnesium

    Cofactor for energy production, protein synthesis, and the body’s own glutathione.

  3. 03

    Trace minerals

    Zinc, copper, manganese, selenium. Zinc and copper held in ratio on purpose.

  4. 04

    Vitamin B complex

    B1 through B12 — involved in normal energy and amino-acid metabolism.

  5. 05

    Glycine

    Used by the liver to prepare bile. Also a building block of glutathione.

  6. 06

    Taurine

    The other amino acid involved in the liver’s normal bile work.

  7. 07

    N-Acetylcysteine

    Supplies cysteine — usually the scarcest of glutathione’s three parts.

  8. 08

    Alpha-lipoic acid

    Mitochondrial energy metabolism, and the antioxidant network.

  9. 09

    Vitamin C

    An antioxidant, and required for normal collagen production.

  10. 10

    Glutathione

    A major antioxidant made inside your cells. Here, given directly.

Nutritional tools involved in the liver’s normal work — not a substitute for doing that work.

Component descriptions state what a compound is involved in, not what an infusion will do for you. Supportive ingredients are selected and finalized by Dr. Aronov during physician screening, based on your individual history and clinical appropriateness.

Before you get in touch

Questions about
this infusion.

Will this treat my fatty liver?

No. This is supportive care, offered solely as adjunctive wellness support. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, prevent or reverse any disease or medical condition, and it has not been evaluated by the FDA for those purposes.

The evidence behind fatty liver disease is metabolic — weight, glycemic control and alcohol. This is not a detox and not a shortcut, and Dr. Aronov will tell you so directly.

Why does it take three hours?

The rate is set slow deliberately. This is the longest infusion on the menu and carries the most components. Dr. Aronov sets the flow rate at the start, adjusts it through the session, and stays in the room until it is finished.

Can I book a single session instead of the program?

Yes. A single session is $850. The six-week program exists because liver support is not a one-day event, but nothing obliges you to start with six. Programs are offered only where Dr. Aronov considers repeat sessions appropriate for you.

I am already under a specialist’s care. Does that rule me out?

No, but it changes how carefully she coordinates. Dr. Aronov will review your medications and will want your treating physician’s written approval before anything is scheduled. Nothing here replaces the treatment you are already on.

What happens before I can book?

You send your medical history, current medications and allergies. Dr. Aronov reads it herself and decides whether IV therapy is appropriate for you and which formulation fits. Nothing is booked and nothing is charged at that stage.

The consultation is $100, is required before scheduling, and is applied toward your infusion if she approves one.

Who actually gives the infusion?

Dr. Aronov does, personally. She reviews your history, calculates the doses, prepares the medications, spikes the bag, sets the flow rate, and stays with you until it is finished. There is no technician and nothing is delegated.

What if I am not eligible?

Not everyone is. If IV therapy is not appropriate for you, Dr. Aronov will say so and tell you why.
