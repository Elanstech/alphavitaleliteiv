Why each component is included

What is inside, and why there are three.

Oxidative stress is the everyday wear that comes with living — metabolism, exercise, poor sleep, environment. The body meets it with a network of antioxidants rather than a single one, which is why three are included here rather than one.

  1. 01

    Carrier

    Hydration fluid

    Sterile fluid. Everything else in this infusion arrives in it.

  2. 02

    Antioxidant and redox support

    Alpha-lipoic acid

    Unusually, it works in both the watery interior of a cell and its fat-rich membranes. It supports the body’s antioxidant network, helping to regenerate vitamins C and E while supporting the cell’s own glutathione.

  3. 03

    Antioxidant and collagen synthesis

    Vitamin C

    An antioxidant the body cannot manufacture for itself, and a required cofactor for the enzymes that build normal collagen.

  4. 04

    The body’s principal antioxidant

    Glutathione

    Made inside your own cells, where it takes part in redox balance and the body’s normal detoxification pathways. Given by IV, it is delivered directly rather than depending on digestion and absorption.

Three antioxidants, because the body uses them as a network.

Before you get in touch

Questions about
this infusion.

Who provides and monitors my infusion?

Dr. Violeta Aronov personally administers and monitors every infusion. Before it begins, she reviews your medical information, confirms the appropriate formulation and dose, prepares the medications, and sets the infusion rate.

From the initial medical review through the completion of your infusion, every stage remains under her direct care.

What happens before I can book?

Every infusion begins with a physician eligibility review, to make sure it is appropriate for your medical history, medications, current health and goals. A $100 physician review deposit begins the process. You then complete the safety screening and medical intake forms, along with any relevant laboratory results or medical records.

Once the review is complete, you will receive an email explaining your eligibility and next steps. If approved, the full $100 is applied toward your infusion. If you are not approved, the deposit covers the physician’s completed review and is not refundable. Completing the review does not guarantee approval or reserve an appointment.

I’m already receiving care from a specialist. Can I still be considered?

Yes. Being under a specialist’s care does not automatically rule you out. Dr. Aronov will review your diagnosis, medications and current treatment plan to determine whether Antioxidant ×3 Reset is appropriate for you.

When coordination is medically appropriate — and with your permission — she will make a reasonable effort to contact your treating specialist. Written approval is not routinely required. This infusion is supportive and does not replace or alter the care prescribed by your specialist.

Why three antioxidants rather than one?

Because the body does not use them in isolation. Alpha-lipoic acid, vitamin C and glutathione take part in overlapping redox pathways, and each participates in regenerating the others. That is the reasoning behind including all three.

It does not mean three is better than one for you specifically. That is what the physician review determines.

Can I just take antioxidants by mouth?

For many people, yes — and for many people that is the appropriate choice. Oral antioxidants are widely available and inexpensive.

Dr. Aronov reviews your current supplements and medications precisely so that this infusion is not simply duplicating what you are already taking to no purpose.

What if I’m not eligible?

Not every patient can safely receive every infusion. If Dr. Aronov determines that Antioxidant ×3 Reset is not appropriate for you — or cannot be provided safely within the capabilities of this outpatient practice — she will explain the reason clearly.

This does not mean your concerns are being dismissed. Depending on the reason, the safest next step may be additional testing, evaluation by your primary-care physician or specialist, a different supportive option, or no infusion at all. The purpose of the review is to make the safest medical decision for you.
