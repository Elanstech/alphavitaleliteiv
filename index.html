How It Works

Five steps.
Zero guesswork.

The sequence matters — it's what separates a protocol from a product.

  1. 01

    Screen

    A structured health screening — history, symptoms, medications, and goals. It takes minutes and shapes everything after it.

  2. 02

    Physician Review

    A physician reviews your screening — and your labs where relevant — before anything is formulated. If an infusion isn't right for you, we'll say so.

  3. 03

    Personalized Protocol

    Your formulation, dosing, and cadence are set to your findings and your goal — documented, so you can see the reasoning.

  4. 04

    Infuse

    Administered by a registered nurse in our Rego Park suite — monitored throughout, in a room designed to slow your pulse, not raise it.

  5. 05

    Monitor & Adjust

    Progress is reviewed against your goal. Protocols evolve as your results do — that's the "precision" in Precision Medical IV.
