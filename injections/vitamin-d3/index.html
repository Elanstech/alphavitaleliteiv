Why get a Vitamin D3 injection instead of taking pills?

When you swallow a Vitamin D pill, it has to pass through your stomach and gut. If your digestion isn’t perfect, your body often eliminates most of the vitamin before it can even absorb it.

What “intramuscular” means

Our clinic offers a high-potency Vitamin D3 Intramuscular (IM) Injection. “Intramuscular” simply means we give the shot directly into a muscle (usually your arm or hip). The muscle acts like a natural storage system, slowly and steadily releasing Vitamin D into your bloodstream over time.

It completely skips your gut, making it highly effective for people who are very low in Vitamin D or have trouble absorbing nutrients.

When do you need this injection?

What your number says about you.

We measure your Vitamin D levels with a standard, routine blood test. Doctors look at a specific number on your results to check your health:

  1. 01

    Under 20

    Severe deficiency (very low)

    If your level is under 20, your body is running on empty. You need a high-dose repletion to protect your bones and immune system.

  2. 02

    21 to 29

    Insufficiency (borderline low)

    If your level is between 21 and 29, your body doesn’t have quite enough to thrive.

  3. 03

    30 or higher

    Optimal (healthy range)

    A healthy, normal target level is 30 or higher.

If your blood test shows your number is under 30, you are a candidate for this high-dose injection to bring your body back into balance.

Dose & schedule

How often is it done,
and when do we check again?

What strength do you use?

We use a powerful, premium dose of 100,000 units in a single 1 cc injection. Because this is a very strong, highly effective dose, we treat it with careful medical oversight.

When do you test my blood again?

After you receive the shot, we will wait about 2–3 months and then run your blood work again. This 2–3-month mark is the perfect window to see exactly how much your numbers have improved.

When can I get my next injection?

Because Vitamin D is stored in your body’s fat cells, your body holds onto it for a long time. To keep you completely safe and protect your kidneys, we will only offer your next injection after looking at that 3-month blood test.

If your numbers are still low, we can give you another booster; if your numbers are perfect, we will transition you to a gentle maintenance plan.

Important disclaimer

The Vitamin D3 injection is an elective, physician-directed service. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Eligibility is based on your blood work and a physician’s review, and individual results vary. If a compounded preparation is used, it is not FDA-approved.

Intramuscular injections can cause soreness, bruising or bleeding at the injection site and, rarely, infection or allergic reaction. High doses of Vitamin D can raise calcium levels, which is why blood work is rechecked before any further dose. This service does not replace care from your primary-care physician or specialist.
