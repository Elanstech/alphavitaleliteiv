Booking & screening

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. All visits are by appointment only; walk-ins are not accepted. Alpha Vital Elite is an outpatient medical wellness practice, not a hospital. Before an infusion can be scheduled, a physician must review your health information to determine whether the nutritional and antioxidant IV support we offer falls within our scope and can be provided safely and appropriately in our setting. If your medical needs fall outside our services, you may be advised to seek care from an appropriate physician or medical facility.

What happens before I can book?

You send your medical history, current medications and allergies. Dr. Violeta Aronov or another physician on the Alpha Vital Elite medical team reviews it and decides whether IV therapy is appropriate for you and which formula fits. Nothing is booked and nothing is charged at that stage.

Is there a physician review fee?

No. There is no screening or physician review fee. Before an infusion can be scheduled, a physician reviews your safety-screening answers, medical history, medications, allergies, relevant laboratory results or medical records, and wellness goals at no charge. This individualized review helps determine whether the IV support available at Alpha Vital Elite is safe and appropriate for you — and, when appropriate, which option may best meet your needs.

The screening itself is a short questionnaire that takes only a few minutes. If IV support is appropriate, Dr. Violeta Aronov contacts you directly to schedule your appointment. The complete medical form and patient attestation are reviewed and signed at the clinic on the day of your infusion.

Occasionally, the review may show that additional information is needed or that the services available at our practice are not the right fit for you. In that situation, the physician will explain why and, when appropriate, whether another option may be considered.

What if I am not eligible?

Here is what happens:

  1. A physician reviews your information. We look at your condition, symptoms, medications, treatments, allergies, and any available laboratory results.
  2. We decide whether an infusion is safe for you. We also decide whether the care you need is something our office can safely provide.
  3. We may ask for more information. Before making a final decision, we may request laboratory results, medical records, or information from your primary care doctor or specialist.
  4. If an infusion is not appropriate, we will not approve it. This does not mean we do not want to help you. It means your needs may be outside the limited supportive care our practice provides.
  5. We will explain the decision. Alpha Vital Elite is not a hospital, urgent care center, primary care office, or specialty treatment center. If you need a different level or type of care, we will guide you back to your doctor, specialist, or a facility better equipped to help you.

We only provide an infusion when a physician believes it is safe, appropriate, and within the services our practice can responsibly offer.

Can I come in if I have a chronic condition?

Maybe. It depends on your condition, your current health, and the care you need.

Many of our patients live with chronic conditions. Before we can offer an infusion, you must tell us about your condition, symptoms, medications, supplements, treatments, and the doctors caring for you. A physician will review this information and decide whether supportive IV care is safe for you and within the services we provide.

Our infusions may support hydration, nutritional replenishment, normal antioxidant defenses, and immune function. They do not treat rheumatoid arthritis or any other chronic illness.

Alpha Vital Elite is not a hospital, urgent care center, primary care office, or replacement for your specialist. When appropriate and with your permission, we may communicate with your healthcare provider. Being under specialist care or receiving treatment does not automatically rule you out.

If your needs are outside what we can safely provide, we will tell you clearly and guide you toward a more appropriate doctor or facility.

Your visit

Who personally provides and monitors my infusion?

At Alpha Vital Elite, physician involvement continues throughout your visit. Your infusion is personally provided and monitored by Dr. Violeta Aronov or another physician on the Alpha Vital Elite medical team.

The physician providing your care reviews your medical history, determines and verifies the appropriate formulation and doses, prepares and starts your infusion, sets the flow rate, and remains with you through completion. Your infusion is not handed off to a technician; a physician remains directly responsible for your care from start to finish.

How long does an infusion take?

The time depends on which infusion you receive.

A glutathione injection takes about 15 minutes to give, with about 30 minutes planned for the entire visit. Some of our signature infusions take about one hour. Our longest infusions take approximately two and a half to three hours.

We do not rush your infusion. Giving certain ingredients too quickly may cause breathing problems, chest discomfort, nausea, vomiting, or other side effects. Your infusion is given at a careful rate, with physician monitoring throughout your visit, because your safety comes first.

Will an infusion treat my condition?

No. Our infusions do not treat or cure diseases. They provide supportive care only and do not replace your medications, primary care doctor, specialist, or medical treatment.

We do, however, specialize in physician-directed supportive IV care. Our approach is informed by published research about different vitamin deficiencies and oxidative stress in chronic conditions such as rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, fibromyalgia, Lyme disease with joint symptoms, ulcerative colitis, and Crohn’s disease.

Oxidative stress means the body is having trouble balancing harmful molecules. Some studies have examined individual antioxidants in people with chronic conditions. However, this research does not prove that our combined IV infusions treat these conditions or will benefit every patient.

A physician must review your condition, medications, current treatment, and overall health before deciding whether supportive IV care is safe and appropriate for you. Our supportive IV formulations are not FDA-approved to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, prevent, or reverse any disease or medical condition. Results may vary, and no benefit or outcome is promised.

How infusions are formulated

What is the rationale for including each ingredient in this specific IV infusion?

Every ingredient must have a clear reason for being included.

Dr. Aronov’s background in biochemistry and nearly 20 years of experience as a board-certified anesthesiologist guide how each infusion is evaluated.

Before including an ingredient, Dr. Aronov considers:

  • What does the ingredient normally do in the body?
  • What supportive purpose does it serve in this specific infusion?
  • Is the proposed dose and IV route appropriate?
  • Is it safe with the patient’s health conditions, allergies, medications, and available laboratory results?
  • Can it be used safely with the other ingredients, or should it be given separately?
  • What risks or limitations should the patient understand?

The review is informed by resources from the National Institutes of Health, peer-reviewed medical literature indexed in PubMed, and guidance from established medical organizations. An ingredient is never included simply because it is popular.

Research involving an individual ingredient does not prove that it will benefit every patient. It also does not establish that a combined IV formulation is safe or effective for treating any disease or medical condition. The final formulation and eligibility decision are based on an individual physician review.

Why isn’t everything mixed in one bag?

Not every ingredient belongs in the same bag.

Each ingredient has its own needs, including the correct fluid, concentration, acidity level, light protection, and infusion rate.

Before ingredients are combined, their compatibility must be considered. Ingredients that do not mix well may become less stable, change when placed together, lose strength, or form tiny particles called precipitates.

For these reasons, we do not automatically place a long list of ingredients into one bag. Ingredients are combined only when they are considered compatible in that specific preparation. When needed, they are protected from light or given in separate bags and steps, with an appropriate fluid and rate for each stage.

This approach may take longer, but it allows every stage to be prepared and administered carefully. It does not eliminate all infusion risks or guarantee a particular result.

Why does the infusion order matter?

Think about building a house.

You would not put all the workers, tools, and materials into one big pile. You would use each one at the proper time.

An infusion also needs a plan. Some infusions begin with minerals and vitamins. Amino acids and antioxidants may be given in later steps.

Some ingredients can go together. Others need their own bag because they may not mix well, may need protection from light, or must be given at a different speed.

Giving the infusion in steps also allows the physician to watch how you feel. If needed, the physician can slow down, change, or stop the infusion.

The order is chosen for careful preparation and monitoring. It does not remove every risk or guarantee a particular result.

Why do so many infusions include antioxidants?

Your body naturally makes tiny molecules called free radicals. A small amount is normal. When too many build up and the body cannot keep them balanced, it is called oxidative stress.

Over a lifetime, the body is exposed to sunlight, smoke, pollution, alcohol, illness, inflammation, and everyday stress. Years of oxidative stress may add to normal wear and damage inside the body’s cells.

Oxidative stress has also been studied in many chronic illnesses, especially conditions involving ongoing inflammation. Think of antioxidants as helpers that support the body’s own cleanup and protection systems. This is why selected antioxidant ingredients are included in several Alpha Vital Elite infusions.

Antioxidant support cannot erase a lifetime of damage. It has also not been proven to treat, cure, or reverse a chronic illness. Each ingredient must have a clear reason for being included, and a physician must decide whether it is safe and appropriate for the individual patient.

Why can’t I simply choose an IV from a menu?

Because the same infusion may be appropriate for one person and unsafe for another.

Cancer care, an active flare of a chronic illness, heart or kidney or liver problems, fever, infection, pregnancy, abnormal vital signs, or certain medications may completely change the plan.

Sometimes the safest answer is:

  • Regular medical care before any IV
  • No infusion at all
  • More medical information
  • New laboratory results
  • A conversation with a specialist
  • A different infusion

You tell us what concerns you. Dr. Aronov determines whether an IV is appropriate and which option, if any, fits your needs.

What does “physician-directed” mean?

It means you are not expected to select or prescribe your own IV from a menu.

Before any infusion, a physician reviews:

  • What you hope to address with IV support
  • Your medical history
  • Your current symptoms
  • Your medications and supplements
  • Your allergies
  • Your relevant laboratory results

The physician then:

  • Determines whether IV support is appropriate for you
  • Confirms whether it can be provided within the scope of this practice
  • Selects the appropriate ingredients and doses
  • Determines the fluid volume, sequence, and infusion rate
  • Personally provides and monitors your care from start to finish

The first question is not, “Which IV would you like?”

It is, “Should you receive an IV at all — and, if so, which approach is appropriate for you?”

Infusion I

Glutathione IV Injection

About this infusion
What is glutathione?

Glutathione is a substance your body makes naturally. It helps protect your cells and helps your body process certain substances.

Why is glutathione studied?

Scientists study its role in antioxidant protection, immune function, normal detoxification, skin brightness, skin aging and tissue repair.

Research helps us understand glutathione. It does not promise a particular result from this IV.

Can glutathione brighten the skin?

Some studies have looked at glutathione and skin brightness. Most used pills or skin products — not IV glutathione.

Skin brightening is only one area of research. Results are not guaranteed.

Why do some studies mention COVID-19 or tuberculosis?

Scientists studied these illnesses to learn how glutathione is connected to immune cells and oxidative stress.

This does not mean that Glutathione IV Push prevents or treats COVID-19, tuberculosis, or any other infection.

Can it be used with regular medical care?

For some medically stable patients, Dr. Aronov may consider it as added antioxidant support.

It is used alongside regular medical care. It does not replace treatment from your primary-care physician or specialist.

How is this option different?

Antioxidant ×3 Reset IV combines glutathione with vitamin C and alpha-lipoic acid.

Glutathione IV Push provides glutathione itself. It is the shorter and more affordable of the two options.

Does every study apply to this IV?

No. Studies may use different doses, products, or ways of giving glutathione.

Research involving a pill, skin product, or different IV formula does not automatically apply to this Glutathione IV Push.

How do I know if it is right for me?

Dr. Aronov reviews your health history, medications, laboratory results, and medical stability before approving treatment.

Infusion II

Antioxidant ×3 Reset

About this infusion
I’m already receiving care from a specialist. Can I still be considered?

Yes. Being under a specialist’s care does not automatically rule you out. Dr. Aronov will review your diagnosis, medications and current treatment plan to determine whether Antioxidant ×3 Reset is appropriate for you.

When coordination is medically appropriate — and with your permission — Dr. Aronov will make a reasonable effort to contact your treating specialist. Written approval is not routinely required. This infusion is supportive and does not replace or alter the care prescribed by your specialist.

Why three antioxidants rather than one?

Because the body does not use them in isolation. Alpha-lipoic acid, vitamin C and glutathione take part in overlapping redox pathways, and each participates in regenerating the others. That is the reasoning behind including all three.

It does not mean three is better than one for you specifically. That is what the physician review determines.

Infusion III

Immun-O-Boost IV Support

About this infusion
Can I be considered for ImmunoBoost while under a specialist’s care?

Yes. Being under a specialist’s care does not automatically rule you out. Dr. Aronov will review your diagnosis, medications, current treatment and overall health to determine whether the infusion is medically appropriate.

If additional information is needed, Dr. Aronov may request relevant records or coordinate with your specialist. ImmunoBoost is supportive care and does not replace or change the treatment prescribed by your specialist.

Will this prevent me from getting sick?

No. ImmunoBoost IV Support is designed to support normal immune function when you are well. It cannot prevent infection or guarantee that you will not become sick.

This infusion is offered solely as adjunctive wellness support. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate or prevent any disease or medical condition, and has not been evaluated by the FDA for those purposes. It does not replace vaccination, antivirals, antibiotics or care from your physician.

If you are acutely unwell, contact your physician. ImmunoBoost is intended for proactive support — not for treating an active illness.

Why every two to four weeks rather than weekly?

When medically appropriate, ImmunoBoost may be considered approximately every two to four weeks, with timing sometimes closer to twice monthly during flu season. It is intended as proactive wellness support when you are well — not as treatment during an acute illness.

The formulation contains nutrients involved in normal immune-cell development, maturation, function and antioxidant defense. More frequent infusions are not automatically more beneficial. The appropriate interval may change with your health, medications, the season and your response to a previous infusion.

For that reason, Dr. Aronov reviews a new screening before every visit rather than placing patients on an automatic schedule.

Infusion IV

Revive IV Support

About this infusion
I’m already receiving care from a specialist. Can I still be considered?

Yes. Being under a specialist’s care does not automatically rule you out. Dr. Aronov will review your diagnosis, medications and current treatment plan to determine whether Revive IV Support is appropriate for you.

When coordination is medically appropriate — and with your permission — Dr. Aronov will make a reasonable effort to contact your treating specialist. Written approval is not routinely required. This infusion is supportive and does not replace or alter the care prescribed by your specialist.

Is this a hangover drip?

Revive IV may be considered for hangover-related hydration and nutritional support in a medically stable patient. It provides IV fluids, B-complex vitamins — including thiamine (B1) — AminoMultiPlex amino acids, calcium gluconate and magnesium. Thiamine supports normal energy metabolism and is an important nutritional consideration when alcohol use is prolonged or accompanied by poor intake.

Revive is supportive and adjunctive — never a cure. It does not treat alcohol intoxication or make alcohol leave the body faster. If you are still intoxicated or have persistent vomiting, confusion, fainting, chest pain, difficulty breathing or other acute symptoms, you need medical evaluation — not an elective infusion. Physician screening is required.

Can Revive support recovery after food poisoning or traveler’s diarrhea?

Revive may be considered after the acute illness has resolved or is clearly improving, when you are medically stable but still feel depleted following diarrhea, vomiting or reduced food and fluid intake. After physician screening, the infusion provides IV hydration together with selected vitamins, amino acids and minerals as supportive care during recovery.

Revive does not treat food poisoning, eliminate an infection or toxin, or stop diarrhea. Oral rehydration is generally the first step. If you have ongoing or severe diarrhea or vomiting, cannot keep fluids down, have blood in your stool, a high fever, severe abdominal pain, fainting, confusion, very little urine or other signs of significant dehydration, seek prompt medical care rather than scheduling an elective infusion.

Infusion VI

Stress & Brain Wellness

About this infusion
I’m already receiving care from a specialist. Can I still be considered?

Yes. Being under a specialist’s care does not automatically rule you out. Dr. Aronov will review your diagnosis, medications and current treatment plan to determine whether this infusion is appropriate for you.

When coordination is medically appropriate — and with your permission — Dr. Aronov will make a reasonable effort to contact your treating specialist. Written approval is not routinely required. This infusion is supportive and does not replace or alter the care prescribed by your specialist.

I already take a B-complex. Is this different?

Yes. This is not simply an IV version of an over-the-counter B-complex. It combines selected nutrients in physician-determined doses and delivers them directly into the bloodstream, bypassing digestion.

The distinction is in the route of delivery, not a promise of greater benefit. For many people, oral vitamins remain a suitable choice. During physician review, your current supplements, medications, medical history, and relevant laboratory results are considered together to avoid unnecessary duplication and determine whether this infusion is clinically appropriate.

Is this infusion a treatment for anxiety or depression?

No. This infusion is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent anxiety, depression, or any other psychiatric condition. Its ingredients have recognized physiological roles in normal brain and nervous-system function, but those roles do not establish that this combined IV infusion improves mood or psychiatric symptoms.

Brain Wellness is a physician-directed wellness service, not mental-health treatment. It does not replace evaluation, diagnosis, medication management, psychotherapy, or other care from a physician, psychiatrist, or licensed mental-health professional. An infusion will not be offered as a substitute for appropriate medical or psychiatric care.

Anyone experiencing urgent psychiatric symptoms, thoughts of self-harm, or immediate danger should call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

How is my customized infusion determined?

Every customized infusion begins with a physician review of your health history, current medications and supplements, allergies, wellness goals, and relevant laboratory information when available. If an infusion is appropriate, the physician determines the components, doses, sequence, fluid volume, and administration rate based on your individual clinical profile.

Customized does not mean that more ingredients—or every requested ingredient—will be included. The final formulation may contain fewer components than a signature infusion, certain ingredients may be omitted, or no infusion may be offered when it is not considered clinically appropriate.

Can I request specific ingredients?

Yes. You are welcome to share which ingredients interest you and what you hope to support. Your preferences will be considered during physician review, but requesting an ingredient does not guarantee that it will be included. Each component must be appropriate for your health history, medications and supplements, allergies, relevant laboratory information, and the overall formulation.

A customized infusion is not assembled by simply selecting ingredients from a menu. The final components, doses, sequence, and administration rate are determined by the physician, and requested ingredients may be adjusted or omitted when appropriate.
