How does a molecule reach its destination?

The question underneath everything It was first asked in a Stony Brook laboratory, of hyaluronic-acid polymers carrying vitamins and enzymes. It is the same question asked at the bedside of every infusion here: what goes in, in what quantity, at what rate, into which patient.
Chapter I The career path

From the bench, to the operating room, to a quiet room in Queens.

Dr. Aronov did not arrive at intravenous therapy through wellness. She arrived through biochemistry, then through anesthesiology — two fields whose entire subject is what a compound does once it is inside a person.

1995 Undergraduate research 01 / 05

1995 — 1999

A degree, and a delivery problem

SUNY Stony Brook · B.S. Biochemistry

Before medical school there was a laboratory bench, and on it the problem of delivery: how to carry a drug, a vitamin or an enzyme through the body to the tissue that actually needs it, using hyaluronic-acid polymer systems as the vehicle.

Getting a compound into a person is not the same as getting it where it is going. That distinction is the reason this practice exists.

1997 — 1999

Nerve repair, at scale

Regeneron Pharmaceuticals · Tarrytown, NY

Overlapping the last two years of the degree, the work moved into industry: research on nerve healing and cellular recovery — the biochemistry of tissue putting itself back together after injury.

Seeing laboratory findings turn into something that changes a patient's life is what moved her from research toward medicine.

1999 — 2004

Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine

NYCOM at New York Institute of Technology

Of every specialty available, she chose the one that is physiology and pharmacology performed live, on a patient, with the results visible on a monitor second by second.

She describes anesthesiology as biochemistry happening in real time inside the human body — the bench question, now with a patient attached to it.

2004 — 2008

Anesthesiology residency

Nassau University Medical Center · East Meadow, NY

Four years of advanced training in physiology, pharmacology, airway management and critical care — the environment where dosing errors are not recoverable and the habit of checking everything twice is formed permanently.

Training affiliates Winthrop University Hospital South Nassau Communities Hospital St. Francis Hospital

2008 — Present

Nearly two decades in the operating room

General · OB-GYN · Vascular · GI · Pediatrics · Orthopedic

High-acuity clinical anesthesia, where the day's work is managing medications, intravenous therapies, fluids, electrolytes, airway function and cardiovascular stability in patients whose bodies are already under stress.

Reading hydration status, blood pressure, inflammation, blood loss and medication response for that long changes what an IV bag looks like to you. Alpha Vital Elite is what happens when that same attention is carried into an outpatient room.

Chapter II The Alpha Vital approach

Four principles, applied to every infusion.

Physician-directed

Dr. Aronov reviews every screening submission, calculates every dose, prepares every bag and controls the infusion rate at the bedside. There is no step in between that belongs to someone else.

Individually screened

Each protocol is built around your history, medications, allergies and clinical context, along with any laboratory results you choose to share. One-size-fits-all wellness is not something we believe exists.

Medically supervised

Vital signs are monitored when clinically appropriate. The setting is private, calm and unhurried, and the physician stays with you — the opposite of a walk-in IV bar.

Adjunctive, never replacement

We complement the work your existing physicians are already doing, and coordinate with them when it is clinically appropriate. Wellness infusions do not replace specialist care, and we will say so.

Why Alpha Vital exists

The attention used in a hospital should not stop at the hospital door.

In New York City alone, hundreds of locations market IV drips as wellness products. Many are staffed primarily by aestheticians or nurses and supervised remotely by a medical director the patient may never meet. Many offer standardised menu drips with no individual review of history, allergies, medications or clinical considerations. Alpha Vital Elite is built on a different premise.

The prevailing model

A standardised menu drip, the same for everyone who orders it

Administered by staff who are not the prescribing clinician

A medical director supervising remotely, often never met

History, medications and allergies reviewed briefly, if at all

Walk-in volume, in an open room, on a fixed schedule

Alpha Vital Elite

A formulation built for your history, physiology and current medications

Prepared and administered by the physician who approved it

That physician is in the room for the length of the infusion

Every screening submission read by Dr. Aronov before approval, not after

Appointments only, privately, at a rate set for you

Most patients here already work closely with other clinicians. When it is clinically appropriate, Dr. Aronov communicates and coordinates with them directly, so that anything given in this room aligns with the treatment plan already in place.
