Question 1 of 24Yes or no

Alpha Vital Elite provides elective outpatient IV infusions, not emergency care. This screening exists so that nobody receives an infusion that is unsafe for them on the day they receive it.

24Questions
One at a time, yes or no. There is no unsure option — if you are unsure, answer yes.
1Ends it
A single yes stops the screening. You will see why, and no appointment is scheduled. It is not a judgement about you — it means an elective infusion is not appropriate today.
0Stored
Your answers stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere. Only your contact details reach the practice, and only if you answer no to all twenty-four.

Nothing here rules out an infusion.

Passing this screening does not mean you are approved. Final eligibility requires physician review of your history, medications, allergies, laboratory information and requested infusion.

Leave your details and Dr. Violeta Aronov will contact you within 24 hours to ask any necessary follow-up questions and, if the infusion is appropriate, schedule your appointment. The complete medical form and patient attestation are reviewed and signed on-site on the day of your infusion. No screening or physician-review fee is charged.

Your details are with the practice.
