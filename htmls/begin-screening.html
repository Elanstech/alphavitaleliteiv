Appointments by physician review only

Begin eligibility review

A physician reads your file before anything is scheduled.

Not every patient can safely receive every infusion. Before you can book, Dr. Aronov reviews your medical history, current medications and supplements, allergies, available laboratory results and infusion goals herself, and decides whether IV support is appropriate for you.

How it works

Four steps, then a decision from Dr. Aronov.

  1. STEP 01

    Review deposit

    $100, taken before the review begins. Applied toward your infusion if you are approved. Non-refundable — see the refund policy.

  2. STEP 02

    Screening & intake

    Your history, medications and supplements, allergies, infusion goals, and any laboratory results you choose to share. Read only by Dr. Aronov.

  3. STEP 03

    Physician review

    Dr. Aronov reviews your file herself and determines whether the infusion you asked about is medically appropriate for you.

  4. STEP 04

    Your result by email

    An email explaining your eligibility and the next step. If approved, it carries a link to book, and your $100 is credited to the infusion.

Four things her review can conclude

  • Approved The infusion you asked about is appropriate. You receive a link to book.
  • A different formulation Another infusion suits you better. Your deposit still applies.
  • More information needed Additional records, laboratory results, or clearance from your own physician.
  • Not appropriate IV support is not the right step for you at this time, and she will say why.

Being told no does not mean your concerns are being dismissed. Depending on the reason, the safest next step may be additional testing, evaluation by your primary-care physician or a specialist, a different supportive option, or no infusion at all. The purpose of the review is to make the safest medical decision for you.

Before you start

Have these to hand.

The deposit takes a minute. The medical form that follows takes ten to fifteen, and goes faster if you have these ready.

  • Your medications Every prescription you take, with doses if you know them.
  • Your supplements Vitamins, minerals, herbal products — everything, not just the ones you think matter.
  • Recent bloodwork If you have it. A photo or PDF is fine. Not having it does not stop you.
  • Your doctors Your primary-care physician, and any specialist you see.

Not for emergencies

This is not a medical channel and nobody is monitoring it in real time. If this is a medical emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department.

If you are unwell right now, the appropriate step is evaluation by a physician — not an elective infusion. Dr. Aronov will tell you that plainly rather than schedule you.
