How it works

Three short steps, then her review.

Every request is read by Dr. Aronov personally. Allow about fifteen minutes and have your medications and any recent blood work to hand.

  1. I

    Your details

    Name, contact, and the infusion you want.

  2. II

    Safety questions

    Twelve yes-or-no questions about right now. Under a minute.

  3. III

    Medical history

    Conditions, medications, allergies — then the III review fee.

  4. IV

    Her decision

    By email, with a booking link if you’re approved.

  • The $100 covers the physician’s time and is non-refundable, because the review happens either way. Approved, it is credited to your infusion.
  • Approval isn’t guaranteed. She may suggest a different infusion, ask for more information, or advise against IV support.
  • Not for emergencies. If this is a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
