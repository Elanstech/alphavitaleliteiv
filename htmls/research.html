How it works

  1. 01Fill in the screening and medical form for your infusion.
  2. 02Pay the $100 physician review fee when you submit.
  3. 03Dr. Aronov reads your file herself.
  4. 04You get an email — with a booking link if approved.

Worth knowing

  • The $100 is non-refundable. It pays for the review whatever the outcome, and is credited to your infusion if you are approved.
  • She may approve, suggest a different formulation, ask for more information, or decline. She will say why either way.
  • Have your medications, supplements and any recent bloodwork to hand — the form takes about fifteen minutes.
  • Not for emergencies. If this is urgent, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department.
