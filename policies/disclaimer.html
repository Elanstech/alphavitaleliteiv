It is not medical advice and must not be used as a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.

Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you read here. If you have or suspect you have a medical condition, speak with your physician.

SECTION 02No physician–patient relationship

Visiting this website, reading its content, submitting the question form or emailing the practice does not create a physician–patient relationship between you and Dr. Violeta Aronov or Alpha Vital Elite IV.

That relationship begins only when you have completed the screening process and been accepted as a patient. Until then, nothing communicated to you should be treated as clinical guidance about your own situation.

Please do not send personal medical details through the general question form or by ordinary email. Those channels are not the secure route for clinical information; the screening questionnaire is.

SECTION 04Individual results vary

No outcome is guaranteed. Responses to intravenous therapy differ between individuals for reasons including baseline health, hydration status, medications, age and underlying conditions. Nothing on this website is a promise or prediction of any particular result for you.

Any patient comments, reviews or experiences that appear on this site or on third-party platforms are individual accounts. They are not typical, not verified as representative, and not a basis for expecting a similar outcome.

SECTION 05About the research library

This website links to published scientific literature. Those references are provided for transparency and education, and are labelled by study type so you can weigh them yourself.

Their presence is not a claim that an Alpha Vital Elite infusion produces the results described in them. Much of the published work on these compounds studies oral or dietary intake rather than intravenous administration, and often in specific patient populations.
