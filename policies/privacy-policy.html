SECTION 01Who we are

Alpha Vital Elite IV is a physician-led IV therapy practice located at 79-01 Metropolitan Avenue, Middle Village, NY 11379, and operated by Dr. Violeta Aronov, D.O. This Privacy Policy explains how information collected through precisionmedicaliv.com is handled.

SECTION 02Two types of information

Website information includes basic details submitted through our general website, such as your name and email. This Privacy Policy applies to that information.

Protected health information (PHI) — including medical history, medications, allergies, and consultation details — is handled under HIPAA and our Notice of Privacy Practices. Submit PHI only through our secure intake system, not through the general contact form or regular email.

If unsure, ask us before sending medical information.

SECTION 03Information we collect

We collect information you submit through this site — typically your name, email address, and phone number. Our hosting provider also records standard technical data, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and reliability.

We do not create visitor profiles or sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

SECTION 04Forms you submit

Forms on this site are provided by Jotform, with submissions sent to the practice over an encrypted connection.

The general question form is for non-medical questions only. Do not include medical history, symptoms, medications, or conditions. If clinical information is needed, we will direct you to our secure intake system.

SECTION 05Payment information

Payments are handled by a third-party processor that meets card-industry security standards. We do not see or store your full card number.

Our records show the payment amount, date, and reference number. Clinical information is kept out of the payment system.

SECTION 06Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics pixels, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or cross-site advertising trackers.

Embedded third-party features, such as forms, fonts, and icon libraries, may set cookies or receive browser requests needed to function. You may block or delete cookies, but some site features may not work properly.

SECTION 07Third-party services

This website uses a limited number of third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy.

Website hosting
Delivers website pages and maintains standard technical logs.
Jotform
Hosts our forms and receives the information you submit.
Payment processor
Processes card payments and receives payment details, not clinical information.
Fonts and icon libraries
Deliver website fonts and icons.
Embedded widgets
Display third-party content, such as reviews, where used.

SECTION 08How information is protected

This website uses an encrypted HTTPS connection. Form submissions are encrypted in transit, and access within the practice is restricted to authorized personnel.

No internet transmission is completely secure. We use reasonable safeguards but cannot guarantee absolute security. Please submit medical information through our secure intake system, not by email.

SECTION 09How long information is kept

Website inquiries are retained only as long as necessary to respond and maintain a record of the correspondence. Patient medical records are retained as required by New York law and professional obligations, as explained in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

SECTION 10Your choices

You may contact the practice to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your website information, subject to applicable law. Medical-record requests are handled separately under HIPAA, as explained in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

SECTION 11Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information, please contact us so it can be deleted.

SECTION 12Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The version in force is the one published on this page, and the effective date is shown at the top.
