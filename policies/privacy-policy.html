Two things worth knowing up front. This website does not run advertising trackers, analytics pixels or behavioural cookies — there is no tracking to opt out of.

And this policy covers the website. Medical information you provide as a patient is protected separately under HIPAA and is described in our Notice of Privacy Practices, which is a different document available from the practice.

SECTION 01Who we are

Alpha Vital Elite IV is a physician-led intravenous therapy practice at 79-01 Metropolitan Avenue, Middle Village, NY 11379, operated by Dr. Violeta Aronov, DO. This policy explains how information is handled on precisionmedicaliv.com.

SECTION 02Two different kinds of information

Website information is what you give us casually — your name and email on the question form, for example. This policy covers that.

Protected health information is the medical information you provide as a patient: your history, medications, allergies, and anything discussed in consultation. That is governed by HIPAA and by our Notice of Privacy Practices, and it is held to a higher standard than anything described here. It is collected through a secure, encrypted intake system — never through the general question form or ordinary email.

If you are ever unsure which channel you are using, ask us before sending anything medical.

SECTION 03What this website collects

Only what you actively give us. We do not build profiles of visitors, and we do not sell, rent or trade personal information to anyone, ever.

  • Information you submit through a form on this site — typically name, email and phone.
  • Standard server records kept automatically by our hosting provider, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested. These are ordinary technical logs used for security and reliability.

SECTION 04Forms you submit

Forms on this site are provided by Jotform. Submissions are transmitted over an encrypted connection and delivered to the practice.

The general question form is for questions, not medical information. We ask you not to include your medical history, symptoms, medications or conditions in it. If clinical information is needed, we will move the conversation to a secure channel.

SECTION 05Payment information

Payments are processed by a third-party payment processor. We never see or store your full card number — it goes directly to the processor, which is certified to the card industry's security standard.

Payment records held by the practice show the amount, date and a reference number. Clinical details are deliberately kept out of payment systems.

SECTION 06Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics pixels or behavioural tracking. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel and no advertising network following you between sites.

Some embedded third-party features — the form embed, fonts and icon libraries — may set cookies or record a request from your browser as a technical necessity of loading. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; parts of the site may not work correctly if you do.

SECTION 07Third-party services

This site relies on a small number of outside services. Each has its own privacy policy.

Website hosting
Serves the pages and keeps standard technical logs.
Jotform
Hosts the forms and receives what you type into them.
Payment processor
Handles card payments. Receives payment details, not clinical information.
Fonts and icon libraries
Content delivery networks that serve typefaces and icons.
Embedded widgets
Third-party content such as reviews, where displayed.

SECTION 08How information is protected

This site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Form submissions are encrypted in transit, and access to submitted information is limited to the practice.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We take reasonable measures, but cannot guarantee absolute security — which is precisely why medical information belongs in the secure intake system rather than in email.

SECTION 09How long information is kept

Website enquiries are kept only as long as needed to answer them and maintain a record of the correspondence. Patient medical records are retained as required by New York law and professional obligation, which is described in the Notice of Privacy Practices.

SECTION 10Your choices

You can ask what website information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact the practice using the details below.

Requests relating to medical records are handled under HIPAA rather than under this policy, and that process is set out in the Notice of Privacy Practices.

SECTION 11Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 through this site. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.

SECTION 12Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The version in force is the one published on this page, and the effective date is shown at the top.
