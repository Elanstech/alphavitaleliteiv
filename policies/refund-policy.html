SECTION 01All payments are final

All payments made to Alpha Vital Elite IV are final and non-refundable. This includes the consultation fee and any payment for intravenous therapy, and it applies regardless of the outcome of your consultation, whether intravenous therapy is approved, and whether you choose to proceed after approval.

The only exception is set out in Section 05, where the practice cancels.

Alpha Vital Elite IV operates by appointment and by physician review only. Every patient is screened by Dr. Violeta Aronov, DO before any infusion is scheduled, and not every patient is eligible for intravenous therapy. Please read this policy in full before paying.

SECTION 02The consultation fee

The consultation fee is payment for a physician's time and clinical judgement: the review of your medical history, medications, allergies and any laboratory results you provide, and the consultation itself. That work is performed, and the fee is earned, whatever the outcome.

The consultation fee is non-refundable. It is non-refundable if Dr. Aronov determines that intravenous therapy is not appropriate for you. It is non-refundable if you decide not to proceed. It is non-refundable if you do not attend.

If intravenous therapy is approved and you proceed, the consultation fee is applied toward the cost of your infusion.

SECTION 03Eligibility is a medical decision

Whether intravenous therapy is appropriate for you is determined solely by Dr. Aronov, in her clinical judgement, after reviewing the information you provide. That determination is final and is not subject to appeal or refund.

She may decline to treat, defer treatment, recommend that you speak with another physician first, or approve a different infusion from the one you were interested in. Indicating an interest in a particular infusion does not entitle you to receive it. A decision not to treat is a decision made in your interest, and paying the consultation fee does not create any entitlement to treatment.

SECTION 04Cancellations and missed appointments

Appointments can be rescheduled by calling 929 · 201 · 0740 or emailing contact@precisionmedicaliv.com. We will always try to find you another time.

Cancelling, rescheduling or missing an appointment does not entitle you to a refund. Amounts already paid remain non-refundable, and it is at the practice's discretion whether they are credited toward a rescheduled appointment.

SECTION 05If the practice cancels

If Alpha Vital Elite IV cancels your appointment and cannot offer you an alternative time that works for you, any amount you have paid for that appointment is refunded in full. This is the only circumstance in which a refund is issued.

SECTION 06Completed and partial infusions

Once an infusion has begun it cannot be refunded, in whole or in part. The preparation is compounded for you specifically, cannot be reused, and the physician's time has been committed.

Dr. Aronov may stop an infusion at any point if she judges it unsafe to continue, or you may ask her to stop. In either case the infusion is charged in full. Patient safety governs that decision entirely and it is never weighed against cost.

SECTION 07Outcomes are not guaranteed

Infusion services provided by Alpha Vital Elite IV are offered as supportive care intended to complement — not replace — appropriate primary or specialty medical treatment. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, prevent or reverse any disease, medical condition or inflammatory disorder, and have not been evaluated by the FDA for those purposes.

Individual results vary and no outcome is guaranteed. No statement made by the practice, on this website, or in consultation constitutes a promise of any particular result. A result that differs from what you hoped for is not grounds for a refund.

SECTION 08Assumption of risk

All medical procedures carry risk, including intravenous therapy. Risks include but are not limited to discomfort, bruising or irritation at the infusion site, allergic or adverse reaction, and, rarely, more serious complications. These risks are discussed with you before treatment and you will be asked to give informed consent.

You are responsible for giving complete and accurate information about your medical history, medications, allergies and health conditions. Withholding or misrepresenting that information may make treatment unsafe, and treatment provided in reliance on incomplete information is at your own risk.

If you feel unwell following an infusion, contact the practice. If it is an emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department immediately. An adverse reaction does not, on its own, entitle you to a refund, and nothing in this policy limits any right you have under applicable law.

SECTION 09Payment disputes

If you believe a payment was taken in error, contact the practice first. Most issues are resolved in a single phone call, and it is the faster route.

Opening a dispute with your card issuer for a service that was provided, or for a consultation that took place, does not change the terms of this policy.

SECTION 10Your acknowledgement

By paying the consultation fee you confirm that you have read and accepted this policy, and specifically that you understand:

  • the consultation fee is non-refundable in all circumstances except those in Section 05;
  • paying it does not guarantee that intravenous therapy will be approved for you;
  • eligibility is decided by Dr. Aronov and her decision is final;
  • no outcome is guaranteed, and results vary between individuals.

SECTION 11Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The version in force is the one published on this page on the date of your payment, and the effective date is shown at the top.
